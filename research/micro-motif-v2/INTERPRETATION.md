# Interpretation limits and field authority

The authoritative v2 strict-event field is `calibrationSupportedStrictCandidate`;
the authoritative trusted gameplay field is `trustedMicroEventTime`. The inherited
`strictGameplayCandidate` field in the copied onset detector is retained as an
internal v1 DSP flag only. It is **not** v2 strict approval. `internallyStable`
spells out that limited meaning. All current v2 strict/trusted counts remain zero
because the independent synthetic calibration gate fails. Production-validated
real timestamp count is zero independently of the synthetic outcome.

“Retained family” means a candidate with at least four aligned, nonoverlapping
returns, retained for inspection. It does not mean approved motif. The family
`falseDiscoveryStatus`, exceedance and `gameplayAnchorCandidate` fields govern the
later research gates. No current candidate is integrated into gameplay.

Lower family counts do not prove false-family reduction: computational sampling,
feature representation and bounded-gap alignment also differ from v1. The
same-search v1-texture, no-HP and no-chroma ablations are the controlled numerical
comparisons. Return-count stability alone does not prove motif-identity stability,
foreground identity or correspondence to a perceptual musical figure. No external
ground-truth motif labels were collected. Surrogate separation alone would still
require musical review and independent recording-timing evidence before production.

The calibration measures the whole onset detector against generated attack truth,
not a claimed oracle error for an arbitrary selected real event. Simultaneous
components share one generated attack timestamp. Precision/recall failures remain
visible even if the matched subset has low median timing error. The complete
condition results and generation seeds are immutable in CALIBRATION/PREFLIGHT.

Null-distribution `preTimingEligible` describes the **top searched null family's**
prominence/span screen, before independent timing and playable-spacing gates. It
is not the number of approved null anchors. With the failed calibration gate,
actual anchor eligibility is zero for every realization and song. The reported
`searchedFamilyCount` includes all qualifying seed families before duplicate
suppression; it is not directly comparable to the display-capped real family count.
The maximum-statistic screen uses that complete searched set, not only displayed
families. Its finite resolution and unproven surrogate exchangeability prevent
calling its exceedance a calibrated probability or guaranteed false-discovery rate.

External “near/outside grid” values describe a generic pulse proxy only. Actual
musical beat membership remains unknown; the inherited `nearExistingBeat` field
uses that proxy on external records and is explicitly scoped by `gridType`.
No new live baseline or production analyzer evidence was generated for those songs.

The listening tool is private and local. Playback supports later human inspection;
it was not used to tune this frozen experiment. Shuffled descriptors are numerical
nulls, not new audio clips, and cannot honestly be presented as listenable musical
occurrences. No audio or lyrics appear in any committed artifact.
